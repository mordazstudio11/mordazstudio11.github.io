Privacy Policy

How Tráfico Loco handles local game progress and optional advertising.

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Contact: mordazstudio11@gmail.com

1. Controller and contact

Tráfico Loco is an Android game developed and published under the MORDAZ STUDIO brand, operating in Peru. Privacy inquiries: mordazstudio11@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

To maintain your progress, the game stores locally a player name of your choice, scores and local records, unlocked levels, virtual coins and blueprints, acquired vehicles and items, pack history, missions, and sound and visual preferences.

These data are used to run the game. The reviewed source does not require an account or implement online leaderboards. Records are displayed locally on your device.

3. Optional Google AdMob advertising

Advertising is disabled in the reviewed stable build. A future version may offer opt-in rewarded ads, such as watching an ad to restore a life.

If AdMob is enabled, Google and its authorized partners may process technical device information, IP address, advertising identifiers where permitted, ad interactions, and diagnostic data to serve, measure and secure advertising. Ad availability and consent options vary by region and applicable settings.

Please see Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob information. Live ads will remain disabled until privacy configuration and technical verification are completed.

4. Consent and privacy options

When ads become available, Google UMP privacy controls will be used where applicable. A consent request will be displayed when required, and a privacy options entry point will be made available where required by the system.

Declining consent should not prevent use of the core game. Some ads may be unavailable.

5. Intended audience

Tráfico Loco is designed and promoted for users aged 13 and above. It is not directed to children under 13. Advertising must account for applicable protections for teenagers depending on their jurisdiction.

6. Retention and deletion

Game progress stays in app storage until app data are cleared or the game is uninstalled. Android may provide system backups based on device settings.

Contact us for privacy inquiries. You can clear local progress through Android’s app storage controls, which also removes your saved game and virtual-currency purchases.

7. Security and third parties

MORDAZ STUDIO aims to limit information to what is needed for the game to function. Once advertising is enabled, Google’s processing will be governed by its policies and applicable privacy choices.

This informational website does not embed its own tracking scripts or analytics. The hosting provider may process technical access logs.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when game features, integrated services or relevant law change. Its last-updated date is displayed above.
